Computer Engineer

          

            I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Istanbul Aydin University who likes building software that holds up
            under the hood as well as on screen. Most of my work is in Python, Java, and JavaScript using React,
            Django, and Spring Boot, alongside earlier grounding in C++, C#, databases, and networking.
          

          

            My experience sits between hands-on engineering and applied research. I started out installing and
            troubleshooting networks at Tawhid Almas Logistics, built Angular interfaces during a frontend internship
            at Caretta Software, and spent nearly two years across three terms at the Industrial 4.0 Research Center
            working on data analysis, experiments, and prototypes.
          

          

            I learn best by taking ideas all the way to working software, whether that's a Next.js and Firebase resume
            builder, a Spring Boot hospital management API, a React Native expense tracker, or a TensorFlow digit
            classifier. I care about clear code, sensible architecture, and building things that are genuinely useful.
